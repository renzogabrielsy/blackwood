- **L-035**  [investigator]  The Smart Adjudicator's `read_run_source` sheet-reader shifted EVERY date one day early (SheetJS `cellDates:true` builds LOCAL-midnight Dates → `toISOString` rolls back across midnight UTC on UTC+8) — so `46171`/2026-05-29 dumped as 2026-05-28 and poisoned a verdict. Fix (`lib/investigator/source.ts`): parse `cellDates:false` + convert the RAW SERIAL with pure INTEGER arithmetic (`excelSerialToISO`, no `Date`/`toISOString`). The RAW CHARCOAL MOVEMENT date cells carry NO date number-format (`.z` undefined, only a date-looking `.w="29-May-26"`), so detect a date TWO ways (date `.z` OR date-looking `.w`) and convert the serial (not the truncated `.w`); a bare number in the plausible date band (43831..47848 ≈ 2020..2030) renders a dual hint `46171 (date? 2026-05-29)` for the model to judge by column. ALSO: a month-boundary day is split across TWO movement tabs (May 29: 11,210 closes MAY, 10,600 opens JUNE) — read the next month's tab before calling a boundary date missing (the "missing May 29" verdict was fictitious). — *symptom: investigator grid dumps show dates one day early, or a bare serial like `46171` in a date column; or a verdict claims the movement sheet is "missing" a last/first-of-month day.*
